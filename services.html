What we do

Eight practice areas, delivered as one program.

Every engagement draws on the same delivery bench — so your Zero Trust rollout, your SOC modernization, and your compliance audit are never working from three different playbooks.

Threat Detection — EDR, XDR, MDR

Layered detection across endpoint, network, and cloud telemetry with managed 24/7 triage.

Incident Response

Breach containment, forensics, and recovery with a documented chain of custody.

Infrastructure & Network Security

Firewalls, IDS/IPS, NDR, and VPN architecture reviewed against current threat models.

Zero Trust Architecture

Micro-segmentation and policy enforcement mapped to NIST SP 800-207.

SIEM & SOAR

Centralized log management with automated response workflows, tuned to reduce alert fatigue.

AI Security

AI-driven anomaly detection and predictive analytics layered on top of existing tooling.

Data & Cloud Security

CASB, CSPM, and CWPP programs with encryption-by-default data governance.

SaaS Security

Sanctioned SaaS governance and unsanctioned (shadow) SaaS risk mitigation.

Identity & Access Management

IAM, PAM, and passwordless authentication design, from architecture through rollout.

Security Audit & Assurance

IAM audits and compliance checks with evidence packaged for your next assessment.

Secure CI/CD Pipelines

Pipeline hardening, secrets management, and ephemeral build credentials.

Application Security Testing

SAST, DAST, and dependency scanning wired directly into the release process.

API Security

Schema validation, rate limiting, and authorization testing for every endpoint you ship.

Zero Trust Architecture

Policy enforcement and continuous verification mapped to NIST SP 800-207.

Infrastructure & Network Security

Micro-segmentation, firewall, and network redesign for legacy and hybrid estates.

Cybersecurity Consulting

Tailored advisory across IAM, Zero Trust, and DevSecOps transformation.

Secure DevOps Consulting

DevSecOps, API security, and CI/CD pipeline hardening.

Strategy & Transactions

M&A cybersecurity due diligence for buy-side and sell-side deals.

Risk & Compliance

Governance frameworks mapped to ISO, NIST, GDPR, and India's DPDP Act.

Threat Intelligence & Research

IOC feeds, attack-surface mapping, and proactive defense briefings for your sector.
